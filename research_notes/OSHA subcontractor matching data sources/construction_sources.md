# Construction-industry data sources for matching subcontractors to OSHA inspection records

Scope: US construction-specific sources that carry firm names, addresses and trade, plus any IDs, and that could help (a) confirm the identity of a GC-entered subcontractor, (b) link name variants (DBA vs legal name, divisions, renamed firms), or (c) add a trade signal. Research date: 2026-10-04. Where a Socrata/ArcGIS/CKAN dataset is cited, I checked its column list, row count and last-update date directly through the portal's metadata API on 2026-10-04 (the "metadata" links below). Anything confirmed only from secondary or older sources is flagged.

---

## 1. DBE / MBE / WBE / SBE certification directories (USDOT UCP, state DOT, city programs)

### Takeaway
UCP DBE directories are free, cover small firms, carry NAICS work codes and addresses, and most state UCPs allow a full-directory download to Excel or CSV. But the October 2025 USDOT Interim Final Rule (IFR) made every UCP re-evaluate all DBEs, and directories were delisted or shrunk during 2025–26 (Texas delisted every firm). So the *current* directories are a poor, biased snapshot. Historic snapshots matter more for 2016–2026 OSHA matching. City and state M/WBE programs outside the USDOT rule, notably NYC SBS (bulk Socrata, 11.8k firms, legal name and DBA side by side), are more stable and richer.

### Cited Findings
- **Federal requirement / content.** Each UCP must keep one unified DBE directory and publish it online. The TxDOT manual says the TUCP directory lists each firm's name, address, telephone and NAICS codes. — [TxDOT TUCP Directory manual](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/civ/dcg/the_texas_unified_certification_program_tucp/tucp_bkgd-i998993/tucp_directory-i999063.html); [USDOT S6 DBE UCP](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/S6-DBE_UCP_0.pdf). I could not open eCFR 49 CFR 26.31 (it redirected to a bot-check page), so the exact regulatory field list is not confirmed.
- **Florida UCP**: online directory "updated daily". It holds each firm's certification status, its assigned NAICS codes, the areas it works in and contact info, with an Excel report option. — [FDOT DBE Directory FAQs](https://wbt.dot.state.fl.us/ois/eocompliancecbt/DBE_Directory_FAQs.pdf); [FDOT EOO Business Directory](https://fdotewp1.dot.state.fl.us/EqualOpportunityOfficeBusinessDirectory/Home.aspx)
- **Texas (TUCP, run on TxDOT's DMS)**: a "Download Entire Directory" button for the full directory, plus "Download Search Results". — [TxDOT DMS directory](https://txdot.txdotcms.com/FrontEnd/SearchCertifiedDirectory.asp?XID=1105&TN=txdot); [Harris County TUCP page](https://transit.harriscountytx.gov/Maps-Services/DBE/TUCP-DBE-Directory)
- **Michigan (MUCP)** offers the full DBE directory as Excel and the MDOT SBP/DBE/ACDBE directory as Excel. **New York (NYSUCP)** has a "Download Entire Directory to Excel" button. **South Carolina** offers full DBE and SBE downloads. **Wisconsin** offers an Excel download. — [MUCP](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/MUCPWeb/welcome.htm); [NYSUCP directory](https://nysucp.newnycontracts.com/FrontEnd/SearchCertifiedDirectory.asp?TN=nysucp&XID=9145); [SCDOT](http://dbw.scdot.org/dbesearch/DirectoryQuery.aspx); [WI DNR list of DBE directories](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/aid/documents/EIF/Guide/MBElist.html)
- **Colorado** ([coucp.dbesystem.com](https://coucp.dbesystem.com/)), **California** ([caltrans.dbesystem.com](https://caltrans.dbesystem.com/)) and **Illinois** ([IDOT UCP search](https://webapps1.dot.illinois.gov/UCP/ExternalSearch)) run their own directory portals. A third-party guide (not official) says the Caltrans DBE System supports NAICS filtering and CSV export, and that the public directory was "taken offline in early March 2026 during the statewide reevaluation". — [californiaucp.org (third-party)](https://www.californiaucp.org/guides/california-dbe-directory). Caltrans' own reevaluation FAQ (updated 4/16/26) confirms the reevaluation. — [Caltrans DBE Reevaluation FAQ](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/civil-rights/documents/dbe-reevaluation/caltrans-dbe-reevaluation-faqs-updated-4-16-26-a11y.pdf)
- **USDOT's list of state certifying agencies** links to every state's UCP. — [USDOT DBE State Certifying Agencies](https://www.transportation.gov/DBEPOC)
- **IFR disruption (present-day status).** As of the IFR of October 3, 2025, all DBE and ACDBE certifications must be re-evaluated without race- or sex-based presumptions. Illinois finished re-evaluating firms that filed by Jan 31, 2026 as of March 24, 2026. Decertified firms were removed from its directory, and non-responding firms are shown as "ineligible". — [IDOT UCP search page (search snippet)](https://webapps1.dot.illinois.gov/UCP/ExternalSearch). TxDOT: "All DBE/ACDBE firms will be delisted from the TUCP directory during the reevaluation process". Only firms that prove eligibility are restored. The latest update is dated June 10, 2026. — [TxDOT IFR update page](https://www.txdot.gov/business/disadvantaged-small-business-enterprise/additional-details-on-dbe-interim-final-ruling.html)
- **Final rule.** According to a law-firm summary, USDOT issued the final DBE rule on **September 25, 2026**. It adopts the IFR with changes. UCPs must finish re-evaluations by Dec 24, 2026 (one 3-month extension allowed), and DBEs have until March 24, 2027 to submit documents. Removals from re-evaluation are "disqualifications", not decertifications. — [Potomac Law](https://www.potomaclaw.com/news-USDOT-Issues-Final-Rule-on-the-DBE-and-ACDBE-Programs-IFR-Adopted-As-Final-With-Some-Tweaks); see also [PilieroMazza](https://www.pilieromazza.com/dot-finalizes-dbe-program-revisions-key-clarifications-for-current-and-prospective-dbe-firms/)
- **Historic open-data snapshots**: NYS UCP DBE list on data.ny.gov (`pfeu-dsx6`; company_name, business_description, street_address, city, state, county, phone, email, commodity_codes; last updated 2016-12-02). New Orleans DBE directory (`q42h-ptn2`, last updated 2019-11-11). — [data.ny.gov metadata](https://data.ny.gov/api/views/pfeu-dsx6.json); [Socrata catalog result, data.nola.gov q42h-ptn2](https://data.nola.gov/d/q42h-ptn2)
- **NYC SBS Certified Business List (M/WBE, LBE, EBE)** on NYC Open Data (`ci93-uc8s`): 11,809 rows, updated 2026-09-29. Columns include account_number, **vendor_formal_name and vendor_dba**, owner first/last name, address and mailing address, website, date_of_establishment, aggregate_bonding_limit, **signatory_to_union_contracts**, 6-digit NAICS and title, types_of_construction_projects_performed, NIGP codes, three job-experience entries (client name, contract value, percent self-performed, date, description), and BIN/BBL. — [NYC Open Data metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/ci93-uc8s.json); [dataset page](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Business/M-WBE-LBE-and-EBE-Certified-Business-List/ci93-uc8s/data). The LBE/EBE programs explicitly target "small construction and construction-related businesses". — [data.gov listing](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/sbs-certified-business-list)
- Other open city or state certification datasets: Austin "Firms Maintaining SBE Certification" (`uxwx-55kj`), "Firms Maintaining Certification" (`94ap-hnnv`) and MBE/WBE graduates (`ibur-wje2`); Norfolk SWaM (`393b-ph9i`); the Maryland statewide MBE/DBE/SBE directory is only a link on opendata.maryland.gov (`djj3-7sjc`, a non-tabular "link" asset last updated 2017). — [Socrata Discovery API results, 2026-10-04](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?q=disadvantaged%20business%20enterprise&only=dataset); [MD directory link](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Business-and-Economy/Minority-Business-Enterprise-Statewide-Directory-M/djj3-7sjc)

### Inferences
- Many state directories run on the same vendor platform (B2Gnow-style URLs such as `*.dbesystem.com`, `*.newnycontracts.com`, `*.txdotcms.com`, all with "Download Entire Directory" buttons). One scraper or downloader pattern could likely pull many UCPs, but terms of use need checking per state.
- For matching, DBE records provide NAICS work codes (a trade signal), a certified legal name (sometimes with DBA) and a business address. That is useful for small subs and sole proprietors, who are under-represented in ITA 300A. DBE firms are disproportionately small, owner-run firms on highway and transit work: concrete, trucking, traffic control, landscaping, rebar.
- Because the IFR delisted or shrank directories in 2025–26, the useful corpus for a 2016–2026 OSHA window is *historic*: Wayback Machine captures of directory downloads, old open-data snapshots (NY 2016, NOLA 2019), or public-records requests for past directory exports. Current directories will under-represent firms that were certified in 2016–2024.
- NYC SBS is the strongest single certification source found. It gives legal name plus DBA (directly fixing the DBA problem), owner name (helps with sole proprietors), union-signatory flag (cross-check against the OSHA union field), NAICS, and BIN/BBL geocodes.

### Gaps
- Not individually verified: TN (TDOT), GA (GDOT), NC (NCDOT) directory download options, or current CA directory availability. Only third-party evidence that it went offline in March 2026.
- Terms of use and robots policies for bulk scraping of B2Gnow-hosted directories were not checked.
- No source found that keeps a longitudinal archive of UCP directories.

## 2. State DOT prequalification lists, bid tabulations, subcontractor approval lists, and prevailing-wage / certified-payroll filings

### Takeaway
The most valuable finding in this area is **Washington L&I's prevailing-wage Intent and Affidavit data on data.wa.gov**. It is free, bulk, updated daily, and has about 1.3M Intent rows. Each row names a contractor *at every tier* on a public-works project, with its **UBI number, L&I contractor registration number, industrial-insurance account number and address**, the hiring (upper-tier) company's UBI and licence, the project location and city, an expected start date, and (via a sister table) the trades worked. Texas publishes bid tabulations in bulk (bidder names by project, county and letting date; no vendor ID). Several state and city prequalified-contractor lists exist as open data. NY's certified-payroll dataset has project addresses but no company names.

### Cited Findings
- **WA L&I Intent Project Details** (`t9je-9qwa`): 1,325,215 rows, updated 2026-10-03. Fields include intent_id, project_id, awarding agency, **prime_company_name, prime_contractor_reg_number, prime_ubi_number**, contract name and number, projectlocation, city, **companyname, license, ubi, companyaddress, insuranceaccount**, **hiringcompanyname / hiringcomapanylicense / hiringcomapanyubi**, expected_start_dt, omwbe_certification, and primeintent. A sample row shows "GREAT FLOORS" (Coeur d'Alene ID, UBI 602049236) hired by "HALBERT CONSTRUCTION SVS LLC" on "Fire Station 5 & VPD HQ TI", Vancouver, with an expected start of 2022-11-01. — [data.wa.gov metadata](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/t9je-9qwa.json); [API sample](https://data.wa.gov/resource/t9je-9qwa.json?$limit=1)
- **WA L&I Affidavit Project Details** (`9ncw-tqjn`): 1,201,699 rows, updated daily. Same identity fields plus **workstartdate and projectcompletiondate**, apprentice utilization, and the prime's intent number. — [metadata](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/9ncw-tqjn.json)
- **WA L&I Public Works Project Details** (`qp8s-a5uf`): every project where the prime's intent was filed on or after July 1, 2019. Has project_id (described as the unique key for linking other L&I datasets), prime name and UBI, contract type and amount, award and completion dates, and number_of_contractors. — [metadata](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/qp8s-a5uf.json)
- **WA L&I Intent Details – Journey Level Trades and Wages** (`h95x-vpyj`): intent_id, project_id, **trade_name**, job classification, county, hourly rate. Example: trade_name "Electronic Technicians", King County. — [metadata](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/h95x-vpyj.json)
- L&I also offers a public search of Intents and Affidavits (no account needed), searchable by prime, any contractor (prime or sub), project, agency, UBI, registration number and trade. — [MRSC](https://mrsc.org/stay-informed/mrsc-insight/march-2025/intents-affidavits-prevailing-wages); [L&I PWIA instructions](https://lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/_docs/pwia-step-by-step-instructions.pdf)
- **California DIR Public Works Contractor Registration (PWCR)**: a public search by legal name, PWCR number, **CSLB number, DBA**, dates, county and city. Selected records can be exported to Excel via "My List". — [DIR PWCR guide (6/2023)](https://www.dir.ca.gov/public-works/PWCR-Guide-External-6.2023.pdf); [DIR DLSE databases](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/dlse-databases.htm). I did not confirm a bulk download.
- **NYS DOL Contractor Registry Certificate** (`i4jv-zkey`, data.ny.gov, updated 2026-10-04): certificate_number, business_name, **dba_name**, business_type, **business_officers**, address, MWBE flag, publicly-traded flag, issue and expiry dates, debarment fields, "business_has_final_determination_safety_standard_violations", and apprenticeship and WC flags. The description says contractors must register with the Bureau of Public Work. — [metadata](https://data.ny.gov/api/views/i4jv-zkey.json)
- **NYS Certified Payroll Registration** (`w2zp-sf2x`, updated 2026-10-04): an account number, project_name, PRC number, work_category, week_ending_date, hours and wages, job_title, project address, and department of jurisdiction. **No company-name column.** — [metadata](https://data.ny.gov/api/views/w2zp-sf2x.json)
- **Texas DOT Bid Tabulations** (`de7b-7dna`, data.texas.gov, updated 2026-10-01): one row per bid item per bidder, with **vendor_name**, bid rank, low_bidder_flag, bid_total_amount, project_id, CSJ, county, district, highway, project limits, actual let date, DBE goal percent, and item descriptions. **There is no vendor ID column.** — [metadata](https://data.texas.gov/api/views/de7b-7dna.json); [sample](https://data.texas.gov/resource/de7b-7dna.json?$limit=2)
- Other bid-tab sources: Iowa DOT, NYSDOT and ODOT publish bid tabs, and ODOT's bid-price spreadsheets include bidder rank. Aggregators BidTabs.us (free viewer, 22 states) and DOTestimate (Excel/CSV export) exist. — [Iowa DOT](https://iowadot.gov/consultants-contractors/contracts/historical-completed-lettings/bid-tabulations); [NYSDOT](https://www.dot.ny.gov/doing-business/opportunities/const-tabulations); [ODOT](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/business/pages/average_bid_item_prices.aspx); [BidTabs.us](https://bidtabs.us/); [DOTestimate](https://www.dotestimate.com/)
- **Prequalified-contractor open datasets**: Delaware "Public Works – Annually Prequalified Contractors/Subcontractors" (`g7vn-fpb4`: company, address, **trade_classification**, max contract value, expiry; updated 2026-09-29). Illinois Capital Development Board "CDB Registered Vendors" (`3dh9-yktq`: **vendor_id**, name, address, **trades**, certifications; updated 2026-10-04). — [DE metadata](https://data.delaware.gov/api/views/g7vn-fpb4.json); [IL CDB metadata](https://illinois-edp.data.socrata.com/api/views/3dh9-yktq.json)
- **NYC Local Law 44 – Development Team** (`6anw-twe4`): the developer, **GC and subcontractors** for each LL44 city-assisted housing project. Has entityname, **parententityname**, individual names and roles, address, **tradetype**, BIN and BBL. Last updated 2024-11-18. — [metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/6anw-twe4.json). The related "Local Law 44 Disqualified List" (`9s68-zggy`) and "Contractor / Sub Contractor Change Order Report" (`gzvm-na49`, 2021) also exist. — [Socrata catalog results](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?q=subcontractor&only=dataset)

### Inferences
- WA L&I Intents are close to a **ready-made per-site roster of subcontractors with state identifiers**. For OSHA WA inspections on public jobs (schools, municipal buildings, roads), the employer name at an OSHA site group can be checked against the intents for projects in that city with a start date before the inspection. That yields a UBI and licence, which then link to the WA licence data the project already uses. It also gives clean **hiring-company to sub** edges, which reveal corporate families and which GC a sub works for. The `insuranceaccount` field is another stable identifier that does not depend on the name.
- `projectlocation` is free text (often a project name rather than a street address), so joining it to OSHA site addresses will need fuzzy, city-scoped matching. In the sample above, projectlocation was absent and only city was present.
- TX bid tabs suit horizontal and heavy-civil OSHA inspections (NAICS 2373xx): the prime bidder names, county and let date line up with highway sites. But bid tabs cover prime bidders only, not subs.
- NYS Contractor Registry gives DBA plus legal name plus officers for any firm on NY public work. That covers the DBA/legal-name and sole-proprietor-officer problems.

### Gaps
- No open subcontractor-approval dataset was found for a state DOT (e.g., DOT "request to sublet" approvals). These may exist in AASHTOWare/SiteManager exports, but none were confirmed.
- California DIR eCPR (electronic certified payroll), Oregon BOLI prevailing-wage filings, and other states' certified-payroll systems were not checked for public bulk access.
- Not verified: whether Texas bid tabs carry vendor IDs in any other TxDOT export.

## 3. Municipal building permit open data naming contractors, plus commercial permit aggregators

### Takeaway
Several large cities publish permit data with contractor names, often with role or trade and sometimes licence numbers, updated daily through Socrata/ArcGIS APIs. NYC (DOB NOW and BIS: business name plus licence number, BIN/BBL, issue and expiry dates), Chicago (up to 15 typed contacts per permit, e.g., "CONTRACTOR-ELECTRICAL"), San Francisco (a separate contacts table with licence numbers), Austin (contractor trade plus company plus address), Seattle and New Orleans (BLDS with licence number) are directly usable for free. LA's current permit feed has dropped contractor fields, Boston gives only an applicant person's name, and no Atlanta dataset was found. Commercial aggregators (Shovels, Gryd/BuildZoom, Construction Monitor, Dodge) resolve contractors to their own IDs, at prices from about $125/month to enterprise contracts.

### Cited Findings — commercial aggregators
- **Shovels**: Free plan ($0, 500 credits/month, 1-year history), Basic ($599/month, 25,000 credits), Pro ($999/month, 50,000 credits), and Enterprise. One credit equals one cleaned record. The Enterprise Data License delivers every permit, contractor, property and resident record as parquet or Snowflake/Databricks/BigQuery shares, "including fields the API doesn't expose". — [Shovels pricing](https://www.shovels.ai/pricing). Coverage is 2,750+ jurisdictions and 3.65M+ contractors (third-party review). — [ColdIQ](https://coldiq.com/tools/shovels); [Shovels API](https://www.shovels.ai/api)
- Shovels contractor object fields: **id, license, license issue/expiry/activation/inactivation dates, name, business_name, dba, business_type, classification, classification_derived, sic, naics, employee_count, revenue, permit_count, status_tally, tag_tally, avg/total job value, avg_inspection_pass_rate, first_seen_date, address, phone, email, website, linkedin_url**. — [Shovels API: Get Contractors By Id](https://docs.shovels.ai/api-reference/contractors/get-contractors-by-id)
- **Gryd (formerly BuildZoom Data)**: nearly 400M permits, 6–6.5M+ contractor profiles, 2,500+ jurisdictions (about 90% of the population), 25+ years of history, 70k new permits and 500k status updates daily. Plans: Free (2 reports), Core from $150/month ($125 if paid yearly, 50 reports), Pro from $480/month ($384 yearly, 1,000 record downloads, contractor search), and Premium at custom pricing with API and bulk delivery. A "proprietary matching system" links permits, property and licensure. — [buildzoomdata.com](https://www.buildzoomdata.com/); [grydhq.com](https://www.grydhq.com/). BuildZoom itself has no public API; scrapers exist on Apify. — [Apify BuildZoom scrapers](https://apify.com/scrapeworks/buildzoom-scraper/api)
- **Construction Monitor**: permit leads in all 50 states, founded 1989. Third-party comparisons list subscriptions at $31–$1,281/month per market and category, with a REST/JSON API and a weekly SFTP dump through account managers. Not verified on CM's own pricing page. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_Monitor); [Shovels vs CM](https://www.shovels.ai/compare/construction-monitor); [API Evangelist profile](https://github.com/api-evangelist/construction-monitor); [PermitLedger comparison](https://permitledger.com/blog/building-permit-database-comparison)
- **Dodge Construction Network**: a REST API for projects (636,000+ per year, with stage, valuation, trades and spec divisions), **firm profiles and contacts across 700,000+ tracked firms**, and plans and specs. Pricing is custom and unpublished. — [Dodge Construction Data API](https://www.construction.com/apis/); [What is Dodge](https://www.construction.com/what-is-dodge-construction-network/)

### Inferences
- NYC is the richest free city source: a licence number on every permit, BIN/BBL geocodes, and, on electrical filings, insurance policy numbers that link entities across name variants. Chicago and SF give multiple contractors per permit, which matters for site-group validation (Section 9).
- Permits name the licensed permit holder (GC, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, elevator, sign, wrecking). Unlicensed trades such as framing, drywall, concrete flatwork and roofing (in some cities) are often absent, and those are exactly the trades that dominate OSHA fall-protection inspections.
- Shovels exposes a `dba` field and a stable contractor `id`. Its "contractor group" concept, if available in the Enterprise license, would directly address corporate families such as D.R. Horton divisions. The $599/month Basic tier is affordable for targeted lookups, but not for bulk matching of millions of OSHA rows; that would need the EDL.

### Gaps
- No Atlanta open permit dataset with contractor fields was found. Nashville's historical depth is unclear because the layer looks like a rolling window.
- ConstructConnect's offerings and pricing were not researched.
- Not confirmed: whether Shovels contractor-group IDs are exposed through the API or only in the EDL.

## 5. Prequalification platforms (ISN, Avetta, Highwire, TradeTapp, Procore, etc.) and EMR data

### Takeaway
Prequalification platforms hold exactly the data wanted (legal entity, EIN, EMR, TRIR, DART, insurance), but only for a hiring client's own invited vendors, and none exposes a cross-customer public dataset. The realistic route is **a GC-side integration**: the GC's own TradeTapp, Procore, Highwire, Avetta or ISN account feeds the matcher with legal names, licence numbers and EMRs for the subs it already prequalifies. EMR (experience mod) data is not public through NCCI. California's WCIRB has a free public coverage-inquiry tool, and Pennsylvania's PCRB may expose mods.

### Cited Findings
- **ISNetworld** shares contractor data with subscribing hiring clients. A Contractor-Operator sees only contractors that expressly authorize it. — [ISN contractor-operator user agreement](https://www.isnetworld.com/en/user-agreements/contractor-operator); [ISN third-party data](https://www.isnetworld.com/en/third-party-data). A third-party API catalogue says ISN runs a REST API at api.isnetworld.com documented only to customers, with no public developer portal (unverified with ISN). — [apis.io ISN profile](https://apis.io/providers/isn/). ISN's key metrics are EMR, TRIR, DART, written programs, insurance and training. — [EHS Inc comparison](https://ehs.inc/ehs/isnetworld-vs-veriforce)
- **Avetta** offers "open APIs" and ERP connectors (SAP Ariba, Coupa, Oracle, Salesforce) for clients. — [Avetta integrations](https://www.avetta.com/clients/solutions/platform/integrations)
- **Highwire** prequalification combines financials, safety data, backlog, project experience, surety information and company details, and offers an open API plus import/export. — [Highwire best prequalification software 2026](https://www.highwire.com/blog/best-prequalification-softwares-2026); [Why Highwire](https://www.highwire.com/why-highwire)
- **TradeTapp (Autodesk BuildingConnected)**: the TradeTapp API on Autodesk Platform Services has a *qualifications* endpoint (vendors' submitted questionnaires) and a *financials* endpoint (vendor-submitted, contractor-entered and TradeTapp-calculated data). No public lookup by employer name was found. — [NCCI Experience Rating Production Service](https://www.ncci.com/ServicesTools/Pages/ERPRODSVC.aspx); [NCCI ABCs of Experience Rating](https://www.ncci.com/Articles/Documents/UW_ABC_Exp_Rating.pdf)

## 6. Surety bonds, mechanics' liens, UCC filings and court records as identity links

### Takeaway
These records do link name variants (a lien names the claimant, the hiring party and the property, and a court docket names parties with aliases), but there is no free bulk national source for liens or UCC. Liens are county-recorded with very uneven online access. Free federal court data is available through CourtListener's RECAP API. The cheapest bond-related identifiers are already inside licence and permit data: NYC electrical permits carry GL and WC policy numbers, WA intents carry an L&I insurance account.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanics' liens are recorded with the county recorder or clerk where the property sits. The claim states the property, owner, **the party that hired the claimant**, the work and the first and last work dates. Online access varies widely. — [US Title Records mechanics lien guide](https://www.ustitlerecords.com/liens/mechanics-lien-guide/); [Levelset county pages, e.g., LA County](https://www.levelset.com/mechanics-lien/california-lien-law-faqs/california-counties/los-angeles/)
- Commercial lien datasets (debtor legal name and address, filing date, amount, status, jurisdiction) are sold through data marketplaces. — [Datarade lien data](https://datarade.ai/data-categories/lien-data)
- **Levelset (Procore)**: Procore bought it in 2021 (reported as about $500M). Levelset says 250,000+ users have used it on 6.5M+ projects, producing payment and relationship data ("who is on their job"). It is proprietary. — [Levelset/Procore acquisition](https://www.levelset.com/news/procore-to-acquire-levelset-to-simplify-lien-management-workflows-for-construction/); [Construction Broadsheet](https://theconstructionbroadsheet.com/procore-set-to-acquire-levelset-for-m-p360-176.htm); [Siteline vs Levelset](https://www.siteline.com/compare/levelset)
- **CourtListener (Free Law Project)**: a free REST API over the RECAP archive of PACER data. Dockets, parties, attorneys and filings from federal district, appellate and bankruptcy courts are searchable by party name. A free token raises rate limits. — [RECAP APIs (FLP wiki)](https://wiki.free.law/c/courtlistener/help/api/rest/v4/recap); [PACER data APIs](https://wiki.free.law/c/courtlistener/help/api/rest/v4/pacer-data)
- Insurance identifiers in public construction data: NYC DOB NOW Electrical filings list GL carrier and policy number and WC carrier and policy number per firm. WA L&I intents list `insuranceaccount`. — [NYC dm9a-ab7w metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/dm9a-ab7w.json); [WA t9je-9qwa metadata](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/t9je-9qwa.json)

### Inferences
- Bankruptcy dockets on CourtListener are a good signal for **renamed or sold companies**. A Chapter 11 or 7 filing for "X Construction LLC" followed by a new "X Contracting Inc" at the same address is a common pattern. OSHA debt collection also follows such firms.
- A lien filing is effectively a "sub X worked at property Y between dates" record, which is similar to permits for site-group validation. It is only practical where counties offer bulk recorder data.

### Gaps
- UCC bulk-data availability by state and surety-bond registries were not researched. Others are covering SOS sources, and surety records appear only inside licence records (e.g., licence bonds), which was not verified in this pass.
- No free bulk mechanics-lien dataset was found for any major county.

## 8. How construction-intelligence products resolve contractor identity

### Takeaway
Public detail is thin. Shovels says the most: it aggregates permits to a contractor using the **licence number pulled from the permit or Shovels' own contractor ID**, keeps **contractor group IDs that consolidate related entities at the state level**, and links a 1.8M-record state-licence file to permit contractors. Gryd/BuildZoom calls its method a "proprietary matching system". No technical write-ups of matching algorithms were found.

### Cited Findings
- Shovels (blog, March 3, 2026): "aggregate records at the contractor level using license number or Shovels' contractor ID". Contractor fields "including business name and license number, are pulled directly from the permit itself". Shovels maintains groupings that "consolidate related entities at the state level, relevant for understanding whether a prospect is operating as part of a larger organization." — [Shovels: construction leads from permit data](https://www.shovels.ai/blog/construction-leads-permit-data/)
- Shovels Contractor State License file (May 30, 2025): 1.8M+ contractors across 37 states. It maps about 3,000 state-specific classifications to 12 standardized SHOVELS_DERIVED categories. About 200,000 licensed contractors are matched to Shovels' permit contractors, and the file is pitched for "fraud detection and entity resolution". Delivered monthly via AWS, Azure, GCP, Snowflake, BigQuery or Databricks; no price given. — [Shovels CSL announcement](https://www.shovels.ai/blog/introducing-the-shovels-nationwide-contractor-state-license-file/)
- Shovels says it collects permits first-party, including offline jurisdictions via public-records requests, then uses AI to standardize, deduplicate and categorize them, with USPS-standardized addresses. — [Shovels permit database](https://www.shovels.ai/permit-database); [Shovels vs BuildZoom](https://www.shovels.ai/compare/buildzoom)
- Gryd: a "proprietary matching system" connects permits, property and licensure data. The method is undisclosed. — [buildzoomdata.com](https://www.buildzoomdata.com/)
- Dodge tracks 700,000+ firms with profiles and contacts. The resolution method is not described. — [Dodge API](https://www.construction.com/apis/)

### Inferences
- Shovels matched only about 200k of 1.8M licensed contractors to permits, roughly 11%. That suggests licence-to-permit linking is hard even for a specialist, or that most licensees rarely pull permits. Either way, permit data will not cover most small subs.
- The industry pattern is: licence number as the primary key, an internal ID for unlicensed permit names, then a looser "group" layer for families. That mirrors what this project needs: OSHA name → licence/UBI/EIN anchor → family group.

### Gaps
- No public engineering write-ups found from Shovels, Gryd, Dodge, Levelset, Billy, SubBase or similar on matching algorithms or accuracy metrics. Billy and SubBase were not researched for lack of time and sources.

## 9. Ideas specific to OSHA site groups: using site-and-date sources to validate an employer name at a site

### Takeaway
Three free sources can reconstruct "which firms were working at this address around this date" and so test an OSHA site group's employer names: (1) **city permit data** (NYC, Chicago, SF, Austin, Seattle, NOLA, Nashville trade permits) keyed by address or BIN/BBL plus issue and expiry dates; (2) **WA L&I prevailing-wage intents and affidavits**, which give the full sub-tier roster of public projects with start and completion dates and state IDs; (3) **NYC LL44 development teams** and **TX bid tabs** for narrower project types. A permit or intent naming a licensed firm at the same site and in the same date window is strong confirmation. It also gives a licence or UBI that resolves DBA and division variants.

### Cited Findings
- NYC DOB NOW approved permits carry BIN, BBL, lat/long, issued and expired dates, work_type, applicant business name and licence. — [rbx6-tga4 metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/rbx6-tga4.json)
- Chicago permits list up to 15 typed contacts per permit (GC, electrical, plumbing, masonry, ventilation, wrecking...) with address and dates. — [ydr8-5enu metadata](https://data.cityofchicago.org/api/views/ydr8-5enu.json)
- SF permit contacts carry role, firm name and licence numbers, plus from/to dates per permit. — [3pee-9qhc metadata](https://data.sfgov.org/api/views/3pee-9qhc.json)
- Austin links trade permits under `project_id` / `masterpermitnum` with contractor_trade and company. — [3syk-w9eu metadata](https://data.austintexas.gov/api/views/3syk-w9eu.json)
- WA L&I intents: per project, each sub's company, UBI, licence, hiring company, city/projectlocation and expected start. Affidavits add work start and completion dates. — [t9je-9qwa](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/t9je-9qwa.json); [9ncw-tqjn](https://data.wa.gov/api/views/9ncw-tqjn.json)
- NYC LL44 development team: GC plus subs with tradetype and BIN/BBL per project. — [6anw-twe4](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/6anw-twe4.json)

### Inferences
- **Join recipe (NYC):** geocode the OSHA site address to BIN/BBL (NYC's geoclient or the PLUTO/BIN lookup), then select permits with issued_date ≤ inspection date ≤ expired_date. The permittee business names and licences become the "known firms on site". OSHA employer names in that site group are fuzzy-matched against them first, before the global corpus. This can settle the "HAGERMAN vs HAGERMAN CONSTRUCTION" type of variant, because the candidate set is tiny.
- **GC confirmation:** permits usually name the GC. If the OSHA site group contains the GC-entered sub *and* the permit names the GC who entered it, that supports the match even when the sub itself is unlicensed. The GC-to-site link anchors the sub-to-site link.
- **Trade consistency:** permit trade (Chicago contact type, Austin contractor_trade, WA trade_name, NYC work_type) can be compared against the GC-entered trade and the OSHA NAICS. A mismatch, e.g., an OSHA NAICS of 238160 (roofing) while the permit says electrical, lowers the score.
- **Corporate families:** WA intents' hiring-company edges and Chicago and NYC permits for big homebuilders show which division name appears at which addresses. Clustering by city could build an alias table (e.g., "D.R. Horton – Seattle" vs legal entity names).
- **Limits:** permits cover only licensed or permit-pulling trades and only cities with open data. WA intents cover only public works. Private-site unlicensed subs (the hard sole-proprietor cases) stay uncovered. Address normalization between OSHA free-text site addresses and permit addresses is the main engineering risk.

### Gaps
- Not tested: the actual hit rate of OSHA site addresses against city permit addresses or WA intents. That needs an empirical sample from the project's warehouse.
- Nationwide permit coverage for this join would need a commercial aggregator (Shovels EDL or Gryd Premium). Bulk pricing was not public.
